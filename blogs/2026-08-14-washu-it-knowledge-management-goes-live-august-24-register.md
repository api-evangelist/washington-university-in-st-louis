The WashU IT Knowledge Management project, one of the top four initiatives identified in the WashU IT Operating Model, is going live on August 24. To prepare for a successful launch, the Service Management Office is inviting all WashU IT knowledge content creators to attend one of the upcoming Knowledge Content Creator Training sessions. Select a Date/Time to Register: Wednesday, 8/12/26, 2-3 p.m.
