Outlook includes several built-in features that can help reduce inbox clutter, improve organization and make it easier to stay on top of messages and meetings. Pin important messagesKeep high-priority emails at the top of your inbox by pinning them. This can be helpful for messages that need ongoing attention or serve as a frequent reference.
