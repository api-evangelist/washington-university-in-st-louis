Beginning with September sessions, Microsoft 365 Platform team training offerings will move to Workday Learning, making it easier to find and register for courses in one familiar place. August training remains available through the Bookings page. To find Microsoft 365 offerings in Workday Learning, filter for WashU learning content and search for “Microsoft.” Current offerings include eight Microsoft Excel courses for a range of experience levels, from foundational skills to more advanced tools, and Hands-on Microsoft Forms.
